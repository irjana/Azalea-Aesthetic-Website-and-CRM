Hair Transplant

FUE / FUT Hair Transplant

A clear, four-step process from initial assessment to full regrowth over six months.

1

Treatment Mapping

The doctor examines the scalp and marks the treatment area to ensure precise, natural-looking results.

2

Follicle Extraction

Healthy follicles are carefully extracted, typically from the back or sides of the scalp, to minimise discomfort.

3

Follicle Placement

Follicles are implanted into thinning areas with careful attention to angle, depth, and density.

4

6-Month Regrowth

Transplanted follicles establish and grow, blending naturally with your existing hair over time.
